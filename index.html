Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026 • PicSqueeze by KraniazDEV

TL;DR: PicSqueeze processes images entirely on your device. We never upload, store, or share your photos. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous ad information handled by Google AdMob.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PicSqueeze ("the App"), an image compression application for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

PicSqueeze is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect:

  • Your photos or images — All image processing happens locally on your device. Images are never uploaded to any server.
  • Personal identification information — We do not require registration, login, or any personal details.
  • Location data — We do not access or collect your location.
  • Contacts or call logs — We do not access your contacts, messages, or phone logs.
  • Camera or microphone — We do not access your camera or microphone.
  • EXIF metadata — While the App can read and preserve/strip EXIF data during compression, this data is processed locally and never transmitted.
  • File names or content — We do not collect information about your files.

3. Information Handled by Third-Party Services

PicSqueeze uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information for ad serving and personalization:

  • Device advertising ID (with user opt-out capability)
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen density
  • Language settings and timezone
  • Network connectivity state
  • Ad interaction events (impressions and clicks)

This information is collected and processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over this data.

Important: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by resetting your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. How Your Images Are Processed

  • Loading: Images are read from your device gallery using Android's built-in content resolver.
  • Processing: Compression and resizing happen entirely in your device's memory.
  • Saving: Compressed images are saved back to your device in the Pictures/PicSqueeze folder.
  • Sharing: If you choose to share, images are sent directly to the app you select (e.g., messaging apps) via Android's share system.
  • No upload: At no point are your images transmitted over the internet.

5. Local Storage

PicSqueeze stores only the following minimal data on your device:

  • Coin balance (integer) — tracks your available compression credits
  • Lifetime PRO status (boolean) — whether you have unlocked premium features

This data is stored locally using Android DataStore and is never transmitted off your device. It can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

PicSqueeze is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the information processed by our App. Since all image processing occurs locally on your device, your photos are never exposed to network transmission through our App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. Your photos and data remain entirely under your control on your device.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may contain advertisements that link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.
