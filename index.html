Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

PicSqueeze ("the App") is an image compression tool for Android. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Photos and images

All image processing happens on your device. Photos are read from your gallery, compressed in memory, and saved back to your device. No images are uploaded to any server. The App has no backend and no account system.

Personal information

The App does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information.

Local storage

The App stores two values on your device using Android DataStore: a coin balance (integer) and a lifetime PRO flag (boolean). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device information such as your advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, and network state. This collection is handled entirely by Google. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — required to select and compress photos from your gallery.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required by AdMob to determine ad availability.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
